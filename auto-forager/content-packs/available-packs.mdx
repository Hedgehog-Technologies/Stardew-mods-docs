Hedgehog Technologies maintains 17 official content packs that connect Auto Forager to the most widely played Stardew Valley expansion and content mods. Each pack is a separate download you install alongside both Auto Forager and the mod it supports. Once all three are in your `Mods` folder, Auto Forager automatically recognizes and collects the items — no manual configuration required.

<Note>
  Every content pack requires its **source mod** and **Auto Forager 3.0.0+** to both be installed. Installing a content pack without the source mod will not cause errors, but no items will be registered.
</Note>

## Stardew Valley Expanded

<Card title="[AF] Stardew Valley Expanded Forage" icon="tree">
  Registers 15 custom forageables from [Stardew Valley Expanded](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/3753) (SVE), including Bearberrys, Dewdrop Berry, Ferngill Primrose, Golden Ocean Flower, Goldenrod, Lucky Four-Leaf Clover, Mushroom Colony, Poison Mushroom, Red Baneberry, Smelly Rafflesia, Thistle, Void Soul, and Winter Star Rose. Also adds several vanilla flowers that SVE places as forage in its custom locations, such as Poppy, Sunflower, Tulip, Summer Spangle, Fairy Rose, Blue Jazz, Sweet Gem Berry, and Ancient Fruit.
</Card>

## Forage of Ferngill

The Forage of Ferngill mod family is split across six themed packs so you can enable only the categories you want.

<Accordion title="Coastal Forage of Ferngill">
  Registers eight coastal forageables: Anisea Weed, Blue Coral, Ferngill Scallop, Glass Eye Shell, Horn Shell, Spysea Weed, Yellow Coral, and White Coral.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Fruits and Nuts of Ferngill">
  Registers seven forageables (Bramble Berry, Chestnut, Crackle Apple, Fizzling Berry, Nimbus Berry, Rose Pops, Sol Berry), one fruit tree (Crackle Apple Sapling), and one wild tree (Chestnut Tree Seed).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Kombucha of Ferngill">
  Registers a single forageable — Wild Leaf — which is the core ingredient for kombucha brewing in this mod.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Mushrooms of Ferngill">
  Registers 11 mushroom forageables split into base varieties (Black Mushroom, Dew Cup, Muffin Bell, Plump Belly, Stalk Cluster, White Mushroom, Yellow Mushroom) and mod expansion varieties (Ridge Puff, Spring Cap, Tooting Funnel, Woodland Stool).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Roots of Ferngill">
  Registers three root forageables: Bardic Root, Gaelic Root, and Sabi Root.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Wild Flowers of Ferngill">
  Registers 16 floral forageables including Blushing Dawn, Bon Bons, Bright Sides, Buckle Bray, Buttered Bell, Calico Blaze, Chill Bell, Daye Breaks, Fox Shoes, Lola Bloom, Luck Puff, Night Shadow, Ridge Riser, Shifter Bloom, Wish Terra, and Wildflower Seeds, plus the Wish Terra Sapling fruit tree.
</Accordion>

All six packs require [Forage of Ferngill](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/14437) to be installed.

## Downloading the Packs

All 17 official content packs are available as optional files on the [Auto Forager Nexus Mods page](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/7736). Navigate to the **Files** tab, scroll to **Optional Files**, and download the packs for whichever mods you have installed. Extract each pack's folder directly into your `Stardew Valley/Mods` directory.

<Tip>
  You only need to install the content packs for mods you actually have. Installing a pack for a mod you don't have installed is harmless but unnecessary.
</Tip>
