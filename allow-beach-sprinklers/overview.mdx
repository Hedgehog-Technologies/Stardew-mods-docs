The Beach Farm (layout #6) is one of the most visually distinctive farm options in Stardew Valley, but it comes with a frustrating limitation: the sandy soil that covers most of the farmable area blocks sprinklers from watering your crops. Allow Beach Sprinklers removes that restriction every morning so your irrigation works exactly as it would on any other farm layout — no workarounds, no crop-withering surprises.

## What It Does

Each morning when the day starts, the mod patches the `NoSprinklers` property on the Beach Farm's sand tiles from `true` to `false`. This single change is all it takes — your sprinklers wake up and water normally, and you never have to think about it again.

<Note>
  The patch runs every morning automatically. You don't need to do anything after installing the mod — just place your sprinklers and go about your day.
</Note>

## Zero Configuration

Allow Beach Sprinklers has no settings whatsoever. There is no `config.json`, no Generic Mod Config Menu entry, and no keybinds. Install the mod, launch the game, and your sprinklers work. There is nothing to configure or tune.

## Applies Only to the Beach Farm

The mod checks your current farm layout before applying any changes. If you are playing on a farm other than layout #6, the mod does nothing — other farm types already allow sprinklers without any patching.

## Sprinkler Placement on Sand

After installing the mod, you can place sprinklers directly on sand tiles during the day. The watering effect kicks in the following morning once the patch runs. Every sprinkler type — Basic, Quality, Iridium, and modded sprinklers — is compatible.
