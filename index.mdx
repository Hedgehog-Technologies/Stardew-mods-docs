Hedgehog Technologies publishes four quality-of-life SMAPI mods that reduce tedium and add convenience to your Stardew Valley farm. Whether you want to automatically collect forageables as you explore, keep your inventory clean, water crops on the Beach Farm, or breeze through the fishing mini-game, this collection has you covered. All mods run on Stardew Valley 1.6 or later and require the Stardew Modding API (SMAPI).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Auto Forager" icon="leaf" href="/auto-forager/overview">
    Automatically shake bushes and trees to collect forageables as you walk by them. Highly configurable — choose exactly which plants to harvest.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto Trasher" icon="trash" href="/auto-trasher/overview">
    Automatically discard unwanted items from your inventory. Build a trash list and use the reclaim system to recover anything sent away by mistake.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Allow Beach Sprinklers" icon="droplet" href="/allow-beach-sprinklers/overview">
    Enable sprinklers on the Beach Farm map. Zero configuration required — just install and your sprinklers will work like anywhere else on the farm.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full Fishing Bar" icon="fish" href="/full-fishing-bar/overview">
    Resize the fishing bobber bar to fill the entire catch range, and optionally auto-hook fish so the mini-game never slows you down again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
